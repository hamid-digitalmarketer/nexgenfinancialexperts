## 2. Deep-Dive Observations (firms whose sites were reviewed in full)

### Kaufman Rossin (regional benchmark)
- **Positioning:** independence ("60 years, independent by choice"), Top-50 firm, awards, Praxity network.
- **Architecture:** services × 14 industries matrix; locations; ideas/blog; bilingual EN/ES.
- **Conversion:** "Explore Our Services," "Meet Our Team," "Contact Us" repeated; newsletter capture.
- **Take-away for NexGen:** the industry-page matrix (healthcare, real estate, e-commerce…) validates NexGen's specialized service pages; a mid-size firm can adopt the *structure* without the corporate scale claims.

### Whittmarsh (conversion benchmark)
- **Positioning:** "outsourced accounting," proactive tax reduction; specific outcome claims ("save at least 8 hours of staff time every week", "find thousands in tax savings within the first year").
- **Conversion:** "Schedule A Consultation" repeated throughout; prominent phone; free-analysis offer; video testimonials; per-city landing pages (Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton…).
- **Content:** S-Corp vs LLC explainers, outsourced-vs-in-house comparisons, year-round engagement messaging.
- **Take-away for NexGen:** strongest local playbook for lead-gen layout (sticky CTAs, phone-forward header, consultative offers). Note: their specific savings *claims* are unverified marketing assertions — NexGen should avoid copying unquantified promises and keep claims verifiable (aligned with the brief).

### Accounting and Tax Services of Broward County (typical small-firm baseline)
- Classic CPA-broker template ( personal/business/tax/QuickBooks quadrants), phone + fax, plain layout.
- **Take-away:** this is the floor of local competition; a modern, fast, secure site instantly out-positions this tier on UX.

## 4. Keyword & Content-Gap Findings (qualitative)

- **Entity-structure queries** (LLC vs S-Corp, Form 2553): Whittmarsh runs comparison content; generic national sites own the rest. NexGen's dedicated page + FAQ is competitive locally.
- **Crypto tax**: no observed Fort Lauderdale CPA prominently specializing; NexGen's recovered crypto page is a genuine local differentiator.
- **eCommerce/marketplace accounting**: same pattern — regional specialists exist in Miami; thin coverage in Broward.
- **Fractional CFO**: served by national platforms (Paro, FocusCFO) with big budgets; NexGen should compete on *local founder relationship + full back-office bundling* (CFO + bookkeeping + tax in one firm), not on platform scale.
- **Healthcare**: several FL CPAs target medical practices (Koffler Adorno in Hollywood; JMCO concierge medical nationally). NexGen's recovered healthcare positioning should be sharpened around *local* practices (dental, medical, wellness) once confirmed.
- **"Accounting services fort lauderdale"-class terms**: contested by directories (Yelp/Thumbtack) + the firms above; winnable through the dedicated landing page + Google Business Profile + reviews.
