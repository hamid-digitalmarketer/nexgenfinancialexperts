# Content Strategy — Messaging & Editorial Roadmap

## 1. Brand Position (validated)

> **"Practical accounting, tax, audit, and business advisory support that helps organizations maintain financial clarity, strengthen compliance, and make informed business decisions."**

Validation: every claim on the rebuilt site maps to a service the firm actually lists (recovered from its own live pages) or to verifiable public facts (Fort Lauderdale address, real phone/WhatsApp/email, real social/Yelp profiles). **No invented credentials:** the site says "CPA services" (a service description, as the original did) and never claims licensure, certifications, client counts, or results that were not in evidence.

### Audience & message matrix
| Audience | Pain point | Message on site |
|---|---|---|
| Small/mid-sized business owners | Messy books, tax surprises | "Clean books, on-time filings, year-round planning" |
| Startups / founders | Cash discipline, fundraising readiness | "Executive-level finance without the full-time cost" |
| Real estate investors | Property-level visibility, depreciation | "Know which properties make money" |
| E-commerce sellers | Multi-state sales tax, channel chaos | "Every platform reconciled, every state covered" |
| Crypto investors | Compliance fear | "Clear, legal, and as efficient as possible" |
| Healthcare practices | Compliance + benchmarking | "Audit and advisory support for healthcare organizations" |
| Non-profits | Audit/compliance duties | "Independent, organized, audit-ready" |

### Tone
Professional, plain-spoken, reassuring. Short sentences. Numbers when real, adjectives when earned. No hype, no fear-mongering, no unverifiable promises (deliberate contrast with some competitor claims observed in research).

## 2. Page Messaging Inventory (implemented)

- **Home:** hero = recovered tagline; 6 service pillars; industries; 3 trust reasons; featured services; about teaser; testimonials (flagged); FAQs; CTA.
- **About:** recovered story/mission/values; "We don't just balance books — we build confidence."
- **Services hub:** "Specialist support, one trusted team."
- **Service pages (12):** recovered hero + offerings + audience + "why", **plus** new FAQs written to answer real pre-sales objections (switching accountants, deadlines, data security, fees avoided — no invented pricing).
- **Contact:** response expectations, security note (never send credentials), hours, map.
- **Insights:** educational positioning + disclaimer.

**Fact-checking rules (enforced for all future content):** tax/audit claims cite IRS.gov, Florida DOR, or statute; every article carries the educational-content disclaimer (already on the site); jurisdiction stated explicitly; numbers only from cited sources.

**Content operations:** draft by AI-assisted research is acceptable, but a human with subject knowledge must review before publish; no bulk unreviewed generation (brief requirement).

## 4. Trust & Compliance Content

- Privacy Policy + Terms of Use shipped as **drafts for legal review** (flagged on-page).
- No fake counters, fake badges, or purchased-review widgets.
- Testimonials retained from the original site pending owner verification; a "verified review" pattern (direct link to Yelp/Google profiles) is the preferred long-term approach.
- Structured data limited to content actually on the page (Organization/ProfessionalService, Service, Article, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList) — no invented ratings.
